How it works

Property Management Solutions

01

On-time rent collection

  • Tenants pay by card, so rent is collected on the due date and anything still open stays listed.
02

Safe data storage

  • Contracts, IDs, and receipts are locked to the unit, and only the right role can open them.
03

Autopay

  • A tenant enrolls once, and the card is collected on the due date through a provider in Oman.
04

Maintenance monitoring

  • A request is reported, assigned, and closed, so the board can see every job from open to done.
05

Governance

  • Roles decide who can act, every change stays on record, and the company and payment gateway sit in Oman.
06 · Ahead

Smart building

  • Cameras, access, and meters join this same platform later, when the tower is ready, not as a second system on day one.
